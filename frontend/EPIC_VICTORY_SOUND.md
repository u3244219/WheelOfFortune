## New Sound Structure

### 1. **Opening Flourish** (0-300ms)
**Quick ascending triplet** - builds anticipation
- G (392 Hz) - 80ms - Square wave
- A (440 Hz) - 80ms - Square wave  
- B (494 Hz) - 80ms - Square wave
- **Effect:** Drum roll energy! 🥁

### 2. **Main Melody** (300-1250ms)
**Triumphant ascending C major scale**
- C (523 Hz) - 200ms - Sine wave (pure tone)
- E (659 Hz) - 200ms - Sine wave
- G (784 Hz) - 250ms - Sine wave
- High C (1047 Hz) - 300ms - Sine wave (getting louder!)
- **Effect:** Classic victory march! 🎺

### 3. **Victory Chord** (1250-1750ms)
**Multiple notes played TOGETHER** - rich harmonic sound
- C + E + G + High C (all at once!)
- 500ms duration
- Creates a FULL CHORD like an orchestra!
- **Effect:** Triumphant climax! 🎊

### 4. **Finale** (1800-2200ms)
**Soaring high notes** - climactic ending
- High E (1319 Hz) - 150ms - Bright and exciting
- High G (1568 Hz) - 250ms - FINAL NOTE!
- **Effect:** Fireworks finish! 🎆
